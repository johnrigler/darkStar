# Dark Star: Church of the Nones / Soirentz Bridge
Date: 2026-10-05

Dark Star should not become the implementation repository for Soirism or Soirentz.

The implementation belongs in the Soirentz project.

Dark Star only carries the philosophical and narrative bridge.

## The dark quarter

Dark Star is the dark/lunar quarter of the larger cycle.

It concerns memory, persistence, death, institutional failure, succession, religion, abandoned authority, and what remains after people move on.

That naturally touches the question of what replaces inherited institutions when affiliation collapses.

## Church of the Nones

"Church of the Nones" is a useful frame for people who leave conventional denominations without necessarily abandoning the social functions churches historically carried.

The question is not merely whether people still believe.

The question is what happens to:

- gathering;
- ritual;
- memory;
- service;
- mentorship;
- institutional continuity;
- burial and memorial practice;
- compensated stewardship;
- succession.

## Soirentz

Soirentz can be understood as the CertLedger of religion.

It does not decide theological truth.

It records and preserves institutional claims, credentials, governance, succession, work, relationships, attestations, and evidence.

That makes it relevant to Dark Star because the larger project repeatedly asks the same question:

> What continues operating after the person or institution that originally gave it meaning is gone?

Soirentz answers that question in one domain: religion and quasi-religious institutions.

The implementation belongs elsewhere. The philosophical resonance belongs here.
