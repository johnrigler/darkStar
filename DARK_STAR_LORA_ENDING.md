# Dark Star LoRa Ending

This note preserves the final-image idea developed after the 2026-09-10 narrative delta.

## Final movement

Daisy does not end the story at the wealthy retreat.

She creates **M64**, but the novel continues into a physical deployment scene. Daisy and **Johnny Ravencoin** leave the retreat and move into the mountains/desert. They camp there and install **LoRa (Long Range) radio repeaters**.

The repeaters are not generic telecommunications scenery. Their purpose is to provide a long-range radio path for carrying **M64** through the landscape.

The final progression is therefore:

**wealthy retreat -> Daisy steps away -> M64 is created -> Daisy + Johnny leave -> mountains/desert -> camping -> LoRa repeaters are deployed -> M64 can move over long-range radio**

## Why this matters

The ending converts the project's field-kit idea into infrastructure.

The earlier practical kit is:

**smartphone + paper + stickers + QR/ledger artifacts**

The closing scene adds a communications layer that does not have to depend entirely on conventional Internet access:

**smartphone/paper field kit + M64 + LoRa**

Daisy and Johnny are not escaping into a pastoral wilderness. They are carrying an encoding/tooling system back into the physical landscape and extending its range.

This keeps the apocalypse/transition theme subordinate to navigation and toolmaking. The characters do not solve the apocalypse. They deploy something that continues to function across changing conditions.

## Closing image

A useful final image is not a grand revelation but two people camping in the mountains, physically placing small long-range radio repeaters across the desert while the M64 system they built begins to have a route through that terrain.

The comic can end with infrastructure being installed rather than with an explanation of what the world has become.
