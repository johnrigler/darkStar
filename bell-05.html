NOTES — PANEL FIVE

The boy is back on top of his box. He rings the bell while reading from
his paper, and the people are now excited by the message. They behave as
if the bell made them excited, rather than the beer that accompanied the
bell before. This is absurd, but the confusion drives the story.

The crowd is also anachronistic: people in modern suits occupy what
otherwise appears to be a medieval place.
