Luke

Luke begins by refusing to stay Luke. The name keeps turning into nearby sounds: luck, look, looks, like. The chapter's logic can follow those collisions. A word does not merely describe the next event; sometimes it manufactures the next event because the mouth found the rhyme first.

LUKE LUCK LOOK LOOKS LIKE LIKE LUCK LOOK, LUKE

The Dr. Seuss pressure belongs in the mechanism, not as a parody pasted on top. Rhyme, repetition, visual similarity and nonsense syntax become temporary laws of physics. Typography can widen, shrink, stagger, double back, or march down the page.

Purpose

Mark destabilizes identity with taxonomy. Luke destabilizes causality with sound. A reader can feel the rule operating without being given an explanation of why the world now behaves this way.

By the end, prose is no longer a transparent window. It is an instrument being played.
