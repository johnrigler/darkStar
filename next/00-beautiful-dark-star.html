Beautiful
(Dark Star)

Before the technical argument begins, the title points elsewhere.

“Dark star crashes / Pouring its light into ashes”

Robert Hunter’s lyric for the Grateful Dead’s “Dark Star” gives this project one of its thresholds: collapse does not end the image. Light is redistributed through the wreckage.

The 18 February 1971 passage later called the Beautiful Jam emerged inside a “Dark Star” performance. That is the parenthetical sense intended here: not an alternate official title, but a reminder that disintegration can become transition rather than merely loss.

This Dark Star is also Saturn, winter, age, recurrence, and the problem of carrying tools and memory through systems that no longer deserve to be assumed permanent.

Lyric quotation: Robert Hunter, “Dark Star.” “Beautiful Jam” is the name later given to the 2/18/71 Dark Star passage on So Many Roads. Lyrics · Beautiful Jam note
