How to use this issue

Dark Star is not organized as a book that ends in agreement. It alternates arguments with small operations.

The essays explain why identity, publication, memory, software, and services can be separated from the institutions that currently bundle them together. The labs ask you to touch the machinery directly.

You do not need to buy cryptocurrency, accept a doctrine, or trust this publication. Read-only entry is valid. When an exercise can destroy value or publish permanently, it should say so explicitly.

The printed object is one edition. The source, ledger artifacts, Chisel tools, IPFS objects, QR marks, and later readers are other layers. No one layer is supposed to become the permanent owner of the others.

Editorial rule: if a page cannot tell you what to understand or what to do, it probably does not belong in the spine of the issue.
