Recognition is not adoption

Modern institutions are good at metabolizing criticism.

A warning becomes a book. The book becomes a bestseller. The author gives a talk. The talk becomes a conference panel. Everyone agrees that the problem is serious.

The operating system remains unchanged.

warning
  ↓
recognition
  ↓
discussion
  ↓
publication
  ↓
agreement
  ↓
little structural change

Buying a book about a behavior is not the same as changing the behavior. Understanding a dependency is not the same as removing it. Installing an AI assistant is not the same as reconsidering the workflow into which it was inserted.

This is also a design constraint on Dark Star.

If the reader merely agrees that self-sovereign identity, durable publication, or replaceable services are interesting, the book has behaved like the books it criticizes.

The publication must make another behavior available.
