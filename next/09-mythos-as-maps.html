Mythos as parallel maps

Dark Star stacks myth, history, literature, and media theory because different maps make different structures visible.

Saturn supplies time, limit, age, winter, succession, and recurrence.

Saturnalia supplies reversal: center and margin, ruler and ruled, respectable and disreputable, financial instrument and publication medium.

Vico supplies return in altered form rather than simple restoration.

Rex Nemorensis supplies mortal authority and replacement.

McLuhan supplies the proposition that changing the medium changes what can be said and done through it.

Joyce supplies language that refuses to remain inside one stable reading system.

These systems are not secretly identical.

The method is correspondence, not equivalence.

The mythic layer gives Dark Star scale and memory. The technical layer remains responsible for proving technical claims.
