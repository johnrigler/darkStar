Executable publication

The physical book is boot media.

It can begin with paper and move into a content-addressed or ledger-addressed environment without requiring the reader to treat one website as the permanent home of the work.

This is different from a book with a companion website.

The propositions are testable by using the machinery they describe.

A critic should be able to enter in order to rebut, fork, annotate, or replace. Agreement is not the admission ticket.

The strongest first encounter ends with one small act that survives the immediate interface: a recovered artifact, a signed statement, a bound public anchor, a copied CID, a reconstructed index, or another durable reference.

Without that act, the reader toured the environment.

With it, the reader operated it.
