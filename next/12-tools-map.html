The instrument, not the cartridge

Dark Star is one publication built on a larger collection of tools and conventions.

CHISEL
ledger reader/writer
identity + signing tools
protocol experiments

MOGWAI
media-facing reader
streams + discovery

M64
compact symbolic recovery
language/protocol representation

PASSPORT
personal trail
physical/digital anchors

LANTERN
replaceable hosted activity
games as entry points

PUBLIC LEDGERS + IPFS
durable/shared substrate

The names are not intended to become mandatory infrastructure.

The stronger architecture lets another reader, server, interface, or implementation replace them while preserving the durable objects and rules underneath.

Dark Star is therefore a cartridge for the instrument, not the instrument itself.
