Find one durable artifact. Copy the identifier rather than merely bookmarking an interface. Resolve it another way. Save the minimal grammar needed to understand it.

If you create a public artifact, remember that persistence can outlive your intention. Keep private keys and sensitive personal information out of public demonstrations.

If you operate a server, treat it as a replaceable service. If you teach somebody, give them enough to teach someone else.

Copy this issue. Print it. Sell copies. Give copies away. Regift this one. Modify your kit. Run your own gathering.
