Lab 1: inspect

Goal: distinguish the durable artifact from the service showing it to you.

  1. Open Chisel or another known ledger artifact and copy one transaction ID or readable address.
  2. Open that identifier in an ordinary block explorer.
  3. Write down what comes directly from the transaction: addresses, values, output order, data, transaction ID.
  4. Write down what belongs to the explorer: labels, colors, navigation, ads, interpretation, cached metadata.
  5. Open the same identifier through a second independent explorer or local reader.

The interfaces should differ. The underlying valid transaction should not.

artifact ≠ explorer
record   ≠ interface
identity ≠ account page

No spending is required. This lab is complete when you can point to one fact that survived the change of interface.
