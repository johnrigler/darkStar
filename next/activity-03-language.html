Lab 3: make language mechanical

Goal: separate encoding from financial value.

  1. Choose a short phrase.
  2. Use a local Chisel encoding tool or a documented paper rule to turn it into a readable artifact.
  3. Before publishing anything, decode it back into the original phrase.
  4. Identify which characters carry meaning, which carry structure, and which exist only for validity or padding.
  5. Save the readable artifact as plain text.

Stop here unless you deliberately want a public ledger record.

Do not send meaningful funds to deliberately unspendable addresses.
