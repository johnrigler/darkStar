Lab 4: make a physical anchor

Goal: turn paper into a user-installed hyperlink without putting a private key on the visible sticker.

  1. Create or obtain a random public sticker identifier.
  2. Place the sticker beside a passage, image, object, or location you want to reopen later.
  3. Record the edition or artifact identifier and a semantic anchor describing the location.
  4. If control matters, bind the public sticker identifier to a signer through an explicit signed rule.
  5. Scan the sticker later and verify that the current binding can be reconstructed.
public sticker ID

      +

      =
physical hyperlink

A visible QR is a pointer, not proof of physical possession. Anyone can photograph it. Authority must come from another rule when authority is needed.
