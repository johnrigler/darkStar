Lab 4: tear, trade, bind

Goal: let a random physical identifier acquire meaning through use without pretending the visible QR itself proves ownership.

  1. Tear out or use one removable passbook leaf.
  2. Choose a random QR sticker. It does not need a predefined destination.
  3. Attach it, trade it, give it away, or place it beside something you want to reference.
  4. Record what happened: who has the object, what it refers to, or what rule you decided to use.
  5. If authority matters, bind the public identifier to a signer through an explicit signed rule.
  6. Later, scan or re-enter the identifier and see whether its meaning or history can be reconstructed.

A visible QR is a public pointer. Anyone can photograph or copy it. Physical possession and cryptographic control are different questions.

When this leaf is no longer useful to you, trade it, give it away, keep it as a record, or let it continue circulating.
