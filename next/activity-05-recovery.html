Lab 5: recovery drill

Goal: recover an artifact while pretending the preferred application has disappeared.

  1. Choose one known artifact and copy its durable identifier.
  2. Close the page or tool that originally showed it to you.
  3. Use a second route: another explorer, another IPFS gateway, a local node, a raw transaction reader, or another independent decoder.
  4. Recover enough data to recognize the same artifact.
  5. Write down every assumption you still needed.

Possible assumptions include a chain name, encoding grammar, signer key, transaction field, CID, checksum rule, or protocol version.

What survived?
What had to be remembered?
What was merely cached?
What was actually durable?

A failed recovery is useful. It identifies the layer that still needs to be published, simplified, mirrored, or documented.
