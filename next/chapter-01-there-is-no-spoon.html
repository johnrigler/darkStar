Chapter 1 · There Is No Spoon

Software tools become habits. Habits become architecture. Architecture becomes a worldview.

This chapter follows that capture from ordinary web software through Node.js, frameworks, servers, and the EVM, ending with “Web 2.8”: decentralized primitives wrapped back inside Web2 dependencies.

The point is not to reject the stack. It is to recover the ability to see it.
