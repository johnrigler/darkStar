Chapter 4 · The Dark Interval

The technical problem opens into a larger one: institutions, professions, media systems, and platforms can all harden into structures that appear more permanent than they are.

This is the darkest chapter. Saturnalia, Vico, the Fourth Turning, McLuhan, Finnegans Wake, AI, replication, and institutional mortality become parallel maps of a transitional interval.

The book is not the thing. The thing is the ability to carry identity, memory, relation, and action across the death of particular systems.
