Chapter 4 · The Dark Interval

The technical problem opens into a larger one: institutions, professions, media systems, and platforms can all harden into structures that appear more permanent than they are.

Saturnalia, Vico, the Fourth Turning, McLuhan, Finnegans Wake, AI, replication, and institutional mortality become parallel maps of a transitional interval in which continuity can no longer be treated as a free assumption.

When continuity can no longer be assumed, survival becomes a design requirement.
