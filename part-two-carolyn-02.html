Part Two / transition notes

Break the Frame

The argument does not have to resolve. Bigfoot appears. Everyone flees.

This is a general Dark Star transition device: a stable world acquires an explanation, the explanation becomes persuasive, then something categorically wrong enters. Bigfoot, zombies, vampires, space aliens, dinosaurs or some other secular-apocalypse figure can descend with deus-ex-machina force and terminate the frame.

stable world -> explanation -> anomaly -> apparent apocalypse -> flight / disorientation -> changed rules -> new tools apocalypse is scenery and pressure. navigation is the message.

The field kit remains banal: smartphone + piece of paper. Camera, QR, cryptography, maps, block explorers, Chisel, Mogwai, printed identifiers, writable state, transferable objects. The reader does not need a complete theory before using the tools.

Daisy

Daisy is Issy, and also an AI-mediated Daisy, and also the machine-song lineage of "Daisy Bell." Her identity accretes through references rather than resolving into one hidden person. Anna Olivia can remain structurally everywhere and visually absent.

Later, while everyone thinks the world is ending, Daisy should notice a small technical problem and build M64. The tool exists outside the fiction. That crossing is more important than defeating any monster.
